## 1. Business Overview

Apple Inc. operates across five reportable segments:
- **iPhone** (50–55 % of revenue): Still the core profit engine; ASP has climbed to ~$875.
- **Services** (26 % of revenue): App Store, Apple TV+, Apple Intelligence subscription (launched Oct 2025). Gross margin on Services exceeded 74 % in FY25.
- **Mac / iPad / Wearables**: Cyclical hardware; M-series chips have re-accelerated Mac share gains against Intel-based rivals.
- **Vision Pro Ecosystem**: AUM of internal AlphaStream model on VisionOS developer adoption is tracking ahead of iPad's first-year curve.

## 4. Risk Factors

- **Regulatory Overhang:** EU Digital Markets Act mandates third-party App Store access; estimated $4–7 B annual revenue impact by FY27.
- **China Demand Softness:** Huawei Mate series recapturing premium market share; AAPL China iPhone revenue down 12 % YoY in Q2 FY26.
- **Valuation Compression Risk:** At 29x NTM P/E, any guidance miss >3 % triggers multiple de-rating historically (-15 to -20 % drawdown based on 2022 and 2023 episodes).
- **Vision Pro Adoption:** Headset inventory build in FY25 resulted in $1.2 B write-down; FY26 guidance for VR/AR remains below initial projections.
