## 1. Business Overview

Tesla operates across three primary verticals:
- **Automotive** (~82 % of revenue): EV manufacturing (Model S/3/X/Y, Cybertruck, Semi).
- **Energy Generation & Storage** (~12 % of revenue): Powerwall, Megapack; fastest-growing segment in FY25.
- **Services & Other** (~6 %): Supercharger network, FSD licensing, insurance.

Key swing factor for FY26: **Full Self-Driving (FSD) v13** and the Robotaxi commercial launch.

## 4. Risk Factors

- **Valuation Disconnect:** At 86x NTM P/E, TSLA requires FSD/Robotaxi commercialisation to justify current multiples. Pure automotive value = $95–115/share (11x FY26 EV/EBITDA).
- **Competitive Pressure:** BYD surpassed Tesla in global BEV sales in FY25 (2.1 M vs 1.97 M). BYD's Seagull is selling at $10,000 in emerging markets — a segment Tesla cannot economically address.
- **CEO Distraction:** Musk's political commitments have historically correlated with -3 to -5 % stock drift over multi-week windows (internal EdgeRunner sentiment model).
- **Margin Pressure:** Ongoing price cuts needed to sustain volume growth; automotive gross margin could revert to 16–17 % in a demand-softness scenario.
- **Supply Chain:** Rare-earth dependency; 4680 battery cell yields still lagging internal targets by 15 %.
